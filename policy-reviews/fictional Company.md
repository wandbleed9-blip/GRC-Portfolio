# Apex Manufacturing Ltd.

**Information Security and Access Control Policy**

**Document Owner:** IT Manager  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 01 January 2025  
**Review Date:** As Required

---

**1\. Purpose**

The purpose of this policy is to ensure that information and company systems are protected from unauthorised access and misuse.

Employees should follow good security practices when accessing company information and systems.

---

**2\. Scope**

This policy applies to:

* All permanent employees.

* Temporary staff.

* Contractors where applicable.

The policy applies to company devices and systems.

---

**3\. User Access**

User accounts will be created by the IT department following approval from a line manager.

Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their accounts.

Access permissions should be appropriate for business needs.

Where possible, users should only have access to information required to perform their role.

---

**4\. Password Requirements**

Users must have passwords to access company systems.

Passwords should:

* Be at least 8 characters long.

* Include upper and lower-case letters.

* Include a number where practical.

Passwords should not be shared with others.

Users are encouraged to change passwords regularly.

---

**5\. Remote Working**

Employees may access company systems remotely where authorised.

Remote users should ensure they are working securely and protect company information from unauthorised viewing.

Users should take reasonable precautions when using public Wi-Fi networks.

---

**6\. Company Devices**

Company devices are provided for business purposes.

Limited personal use may be permitted where it does not interfere with business activities.

Employees should report lost or stolen devices to their manager as soon as possible.

---

**7\. Security Incidents**

Employees should report security incidents to the IT department.

Examples of incidents include:

* Suspicious emails.

* Malware infections.

* Lost devices.

* Unauthorised access.

The IT department will investigate incidents as appropriate.

---

**8\. Acceptable Use**

Employees should use company systems responsibly.

Users must not:

* Access inappropriate websites.

* Install unauthorised software where possible.

* Use company systems in a way that negatively affects other users.

Internet access may be monitored.

---

**9\. Compliance**

Users who fail to comply with this policy may be subject to disciplinary action.

Managers are responsible for encouraging compliance with the policy.

---

**10\. Exceptions**

Exceptions to this policy may be granted by management where business requirements justify alternative arrangements.

---

**End of Document**
