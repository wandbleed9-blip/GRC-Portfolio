# Cybersecurity Policy

**Version:** 1.0 **Owner:** Home lab administrator **Review cycle:** Annually, or on any major change to the lab environment

## Purpose

This document provides the overarching governance policy for the protection and security of the Home lab environment.

The policy aims to define the high-level governance of cyber security within the setup.

## Objectives

The main objectives of this policy are to:

- Present the requirements, control objectives and principles for cyber security  
- Outline the structure and roles of the cyber security governance  
- Maintain confidence that cyber security governance meets the environment's defined risk appetite

## Scope

This policy applies to the Home lab environment, its data and information, directly or indirectly, including via any third parties it depends on.

## Policy mandate, approval and maintenance

This policy is approved by the owner of the Home lab.

It serves as a practical governance exercise for a home lab setup, modelled on the structure a real organisational information security policy would take.

## Policy

### Requirements, control objectives and principles

### Risk appetite

Risk appetite is reflected in the scoring thresholds defined in the risk register.  Risks rated Medium (score 5-9) or above require a documented treatment plan and an assigned control; risks rated Low (score 1-4) may be accepted without further action, subject to periodic review.

### Compliance

The owner is required to comply with applicable law and relevant regulations. This policy mandates compliance with, at a minimum:

- Ethical and lawful use of the internet, in line with the Online Safety Act 2023

### Roles

**Owner** \- the person responsible for the data and compliance of a particular area or system. In this environment, the Home lab administrator holds this role for all systems and data.

### Applicable standards

Several standards inform this policy and the wider portfolio:

- ISO/IEC 27000 family of information security standards  
- UK Government Cyber Security Standards (minimum baseline)

### Policy exceptions and violations

Any deviation from this policy should be documented, along with the reason for it, in the Risk Register or Gap Analysis as appropriate. Repeated or serious deviation from a control should prompt a review of either the control itself or this policy, rather than being treated as a compliance failure in isolation.
